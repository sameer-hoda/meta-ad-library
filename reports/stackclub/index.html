They sell one thing on Meta right now: sleep. 7 of 10 ads push the Complete Sleep Stack with a message running unchanged for 7 weeks — magnesium + glycine moves you from “stress mode to recovery mode,” no melatonin. Around that control: 3 creator videos dropped in 48 hours (the new bet), one 7-week-old Hinglish explainer, one lone gut-health test. Zero discounts, zero urgency, zero price anywhere. All trust, all product page.

Their template

  • Hook, never brand-first: confession (“groggy person”), bold claim (“best mg out there”), or proof on screen (research paper, product macro). Caption carries it sound-off.
  • Seconds 3–15 extend the premise: one-week trial, radiator analogy, “made a video before.” No brand intro detour.
  • Middle is mechanism demo: 60 Capsules macro, bottles held to camera, 3D gut render.
  • Close is identity or clean label, not specs. Then Shop now → exact product page.
  • Static = frozen copy, rotating visual. Video = one distinct hook each. 60–100s talking heads, fully captioned.

Second-level reads

  • The ~10-day refresh is a fatigue system, not testing. Same words, new skin — they manage frequency with production.
  • 60–100s runtimes are too long for cold prospecting. These double as organic/retargeting assets; production cost is amortized across placements.
  • No offer + blank title on their best testimonial = lean team, confident product. Speed is the way to beat them, not polish.
  • 70% of the set is one SKU. “Magnesium is magnesium” pressure hits their whole account in the same week.
  • Only one ad describes waking up. Their story ends at bedtime; the morning is unclaimed.

What to do about it

  • Take the morning. Own wake-up outcomes — energy, focus, first meeting. Their narrative stops at lights-out.
  • Fill the offer gap. Trial, guarantee, starter pack — the risk-reversal they refuse to run.
  • Commoditize the mechanism (“magnesium is magnesium” + price) or answer it with a better-named one. Don't fight on milligrams.
  • Match creator velocity. 3 videos in 48h is their new pace; that cadence is the number to beat.
