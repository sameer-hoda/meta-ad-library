Statics = repeat one message

  • Same copy on all 5: magnesium bisglycinate + glycine, "stress mode → recovery mode"
  • Only the visual changes
  • Message unchanged for 7 weeks

Videos = explore new angles

  • 5 videos, 5 different hooks
  • Avg ~71s (59–102s), long enough to teach
  • 1 of 5 in Hinglish, and it's the longest-running

→ Statics carry the message they've already proven. Videos test new hooks without risking it.

5.User insights they're chasing

I'm exhausted, but I don't want to depend on melatonin.→ the core of every static
I sleep 8 hours and still wake up wrecked.→ quality over quantity
Magnesium didn't work for me.→ "wrong form," not wrong idea
Supplement brands overhype everything.→ anti-hype tone, cited studies
Good sleep feels like a luxury.→ make it feel attainable
Immunity boosters are nonsense.→ "balance, not boost" (gut test)

Gaps they've left open

  • No offer: no price, trial or guarantee anywhere.
  • No morning: only one ad mentions waking up clear.
  • Hinglish under-used: it's their best survivor, yet only one exists.
